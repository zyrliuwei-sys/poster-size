## Introduction

postersize ai is an AI-powered poster size lookup and poster design platform. It helps you find the right dimensions for print and digital work, explore poster formats, and create poster drafts with one-click AI generation. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use postersize ai.

## Information We Collect

1. **Account information** — Your name, email address, and authentication details when you create or use an account.
2. **Poster requests** — Prompts, copy, design instructions, selected poster sizes, aspect ratios, styles, colors, and other settings you submit to the platform.
3. **Generated designs** — AI-generated poster drafts, previews, exported files, and related design history saved to your account.
4. **Usage and credit data** — Generation activity, feature usage, credit consumption, plan information, and account actions needed to operate the service.
5. **Device and technical information** — Browser type, device type, operating system, approximate network information, diagnostics, and service logs.
6. **Cookies and preferences** — Cookies or similar technologies used for authentication, language, theme, security, and essential product preferences.
7. **Payment information** — Billing and transaction information handled by our payment providers. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.

## How We Use Information

- Provide AI-powered poster size recommendations and format references.
- Process poster prompts and design settings to create requested poster drafts.
- Provide one-click generation, previews, editing, exports, and saved design history.
- Manage accounts, credits, subscriptions, payments, and transaction confirmations.
- Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve postersize ai.
- Send service notices, security alerts, product updates, and support messages.
- Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
- Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and security incidents.

## AI Processing and Your Content

Your prompts, poster copy, size selections, and design settings are processed to provide the AI features you request. Depending on the feature and the service configuration, this information may be processed by our hosting, AI, storage, rendering, or other infrastructure providers acting on our behalf.

We do not sell your poster prompts, design inputs, or generated designs for unrelated advertising. We use them to provide and improve the requested service, protect the platform, and support your account. Please do not submit confidential information or text, images, logos, or other materials that you do not have the right to use.

Generated designs and basic metadata may be saved to your account history so you can view, edit, and download them later. To request deletion of account data, design inputs, or generated designs, contact us at the address below.

## Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed to operate and protect the service, including with:

- **AI, hosting, and rendering providers** — To process requested poster size recommendations, AI generations, previews, and exports.
- **Payment providers** — To process purchases, subscriptions, and refunds.
- **Authentication, storage, and email providers** — To support account access, file delivery, backups, and transactional communication.
- **Service providers and advisors** — Where needed to maintain, secure, or improve the platform under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal authorities** — When required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain account and transaction information while your account is active or as needed for legal, security, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes. Generated designs and related history are retained until you delete them, request deletion, or close your account, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us at **support@postersize.org**. We may need to verify your identity before completing the request.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, and security monitoring. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

postersize ai is not directed to children under 13, or under 16 in jurisdictions where that higher age applies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new revision date. Your continued use of postersize ai after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
